# Implementation Phase 1a 
Implementation Phase 1a involved setting up the land and drilling contracts for drilling in implementation phase 1b. This implementation phase was completed remotely over Fall 2023.
- [x] Land contracts
- [x] Water testing
- [x] hydro geological survey

## Hydro geological survey
Take a look at the hydrogeological survey if you would like below.
<details>
<summary><b>hydrosurvey</b></summary>
[hydrosurvey](../assets/Hydrogeological survey report.doc.pdf)
</details> 
The main point from the hydrogeological survey was that it is very possible that there is a lot of water below the surface and drilling is a go. Another important point that you should note/know is that most of our yield in the borehole is going to be from the middle layer of rock, where water flows in through the sides underground through cracks in the rock. This section of the soil and rock we had planned to have a screening to let in water but not soil and rock. When we installed the borehole, they messed up the screening, killing a lot of our possible yield.
